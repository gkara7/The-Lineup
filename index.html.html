01 / 09 The Problem

The amateur is the integrator.

The serious amateur endurance athlete — runner, cyclist, swimmer, triathlete — has access to more performance tools than ever before. They use almost none of them well.

"My coach doesn't talk to my PT. My PT doesn't know my training load. My bike fitter has never seen my power data. My nutritionist is a different person every year. I'm spending $15,000 a year on this and it's a mess."

— Composite quote from 25 customer-discovery conversations with $300K+ HHI amateurs in NYC, SF, Boston, and DC.

  1. One coach, one perspective

    Even great coaches are generalists across disciplines. Your tri coach isn't a swim technician. Your running coach isn't a sports nutritionist. Your strength coach hasn't read your power file.

  2. Testing happens in a vacuum

    VO2 max at one clinic, DEXA at another, lactate threshold somewhere else. Nobody synthesizes it into training. The athlete tries — and fails.

  3. Injury management is reactive

    PT is something you do when something hurts. Not something integrated into training programming. By the time the athlete sees a PT, the damage is done.

  4. The high-end gap

    Below $200/month: generic training apps. Above $5K/month: a personal coach. Nothing serious in between for the athlete who wants pro-grade coordination at amateur scale.

— RISK REGISTER What kills us

The four real risks — and how we kill each one.

— 01 / HIGHEST

We stop shipping content.

Mitigation: Editor on retainer from day one. Six-episode content buffer pre-launch. Weekly cadence with brother as accountability partner. Public commitment to the audience. If we miss two weeks, we owe the audience an explanation.
— 02 / HIGH

Coaching quality drifts as we scale.

Mitigation: Head Coach manages every coach hire. Methodology codified in a living document. Monthly athlete NPS. Coaches who underperform are terminated. Brother spends 30%+ of time on coach quality through month 24.
— 03 / MEDIUM

The integrated product is too operationally complex.

Mitigation: Test on 15–25 Founding Athletes in Phase 1 before scaling. Athlete dashboard built before public launch. Brother's full-time role is precisely this coordination. If unit economics break at 50 athletes, we re-tier before scaling further.
— 04 / FOUNDATIONAL

Sibling-founder dynamic breaks.

Mitigation: Founder agreement with explicit decision domains, buy-sell, dispute resolution, exit. An advisor we both respect as a tiebreaker. Quarterly "founder offsite" — written agenda, hard conversations on the calendar. Brother and sibling roles kept separate from professional roles.
